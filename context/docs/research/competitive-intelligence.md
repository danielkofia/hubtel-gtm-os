# Competitive Intelligence: Hubtel

Independent educational research, not affiliated with Hubtel. Access date: 2026-11-13. Source IDs (S#) refer to [source-register.md](source-register.md).

## Executive summary

Hubtel competes in two arenas. In **payment acceptance**, the direct competitors are other Bank of Ghana PSP Enhanced licensees: Paystack, Flutterwave, ExpressPay, Cellulant, Fincra and Payswitch (S16). In **business operations software** (reconciliation, multi-branch control, staff approvals), the real alternatives are spreadsheets, POS vendors, accounting tools, bank apps and mobile money merchant tools, which are indirect. Public pricing evidence is weak and conflicting. This research cannot yet rank Hubtel on price, features or share.

## Findings

Other names in third-party lists that I **could not confirm as BoG-listed**: Slydepay, Zeepay, iPay, DPO, Theteller (S16, S30). Absence from the page does not prove they are unlicensed.

### 2. Indirect alternatives (different product, same job)

These are **Assumptions** from Hubtel's own problem statements (S2, S3). No source was gathered for each:
- Direct mobile money merchant accounts and bank apps (the owner "checks MTN, checks the bank app").
- Spreadsheets per branch ("4 branches, 4 spreadsheets").
- Generic POS systems and card readers.
- Accounting and ERP software.
- Cash.

Hubtel's own copy treats the **status quo (manual reconciliation)** as the main rival, not another gateway.

### 3. Adjacent consumer competitors (not relevant to a B2B exercise)

Bolt Food, Chowdeck, and (until 2024) Glovo and Jumia Food in food delivery (S25). Mentioned for completeness only.

### 5. Differentiation signals visible in Hubtel's own material

- Positions up-market: "for scale-ready businesses... not just collect mobile money" (S1).
- Sells control and visibility (payroll approvals, branch dashboard, audit log) rather than raw acceptance (S3).
- Heritage in utilities and bills (ECG, water, TV, airtime) via API (S5).
- Consumer apps give an integrated payer base (S1; strength is **Assumption**).
- Local focus: it closed Kenya, Nigeria and Cameroon offices to concentrate on Ghana (CEO account, search summary). Competitors named above are multi-country or pan-African.

These are Hubtel's claims. Independent validation against competitor feature sets was **not** done.

## Confidence level

- Who the licensed direct competitors are: **High** (regulator list).
- Competitor positioning and features: **Low** (third-party blogs, some affiliate).
- Pricing: **Low**.
- Market share: **Not available**. Hubtel CEO claims about 12% of volume (S28) is unverified, and no competitor share data was found.

## Knowledge gaps

- Official pricing and feature pages of Paystack Ghana, Flutterwave Ghana, ExpressPay, Cellulant, Payswitch.
- Whether any competitor sells multi-branch reconciliation and payout approvals.
- Hubtel's current fee page.
- Local POS and accounting alternatives used by Ghanaian SMEs.
- Competitor case studies in utilities, insurance and gaming.

## GTM implications

1. Do not position on "we accept mobile money and cards": every licensed rival does. The visible Hubtel angle is control and reconciliation across branches and channels.
2. The most useful competitive intelligence for a campaign is **how buyers manage this today** (manual process), which needs customer-level evidence.
3. Avoid fee comparisons in any copy until official pricing is confirmed.

## What changed

- **No change to market sizing or competitor pricing.** The Bank of Ghana 2025 Payment Systems report (S17) was **not opened** in this pass, and no competitor's official pricing was retrieved. All Pass 1 market and competitor findings keep their original confidence levels.
- **Hubtel's own fee statement is now sourced.** Hubtel states its standard merchant fee is 1.95% per transaction, of which 1% goes to mobile money and card scheme providers and Hubtel keeps 0.95% (S40, S41, 2024). This upgrades the earlier C-grade listing of 1.95% (S29) to a Hubtel statement. It is a 2024 statement made in the ECG context, and Hubtel's fee page (S52) returned no content, so the current merchant rate card is **still unverified**.
- **Hubtel's settlement claim.** Hubtel says its merchant balance is credited hourly, every day, and that major Ghanaian gateways typically settle next business day (T+1), making Hubtel "roughly 96% faster" (S48). This is a **Hubtel comparison claim**. Competitor settlement terms were not checked against official sources. One Pass 1 blog source said Paystack and Flutterwave settle T+1 (C-grade), which is consistent but not verified.
- **Market share.** The CEO said about 12% of transaction volume, which is roughly 2% of value (S37, S39). A separate interview says "roughly 10 to 12% of the Ghanaian market" with no metric (S36). Both are Hubtel statements. No independent share data found.
- **Value vs volume.** The 12% volume vs 2% value pair suggests Hubtel's average transaction is small relative to the market. Inference only.
